**Berry version:** scatter about 150 g of berries over the batter in the pan before baking.

**Double batch:** heat a 23 × 33 cm baking dish, butter it with 56 g melted butter and bake for 20–23 minutes.

You can make the batter ahead and keep it in the fridge. Whisk it before using, and don't pour cold batter into hot glassware.
