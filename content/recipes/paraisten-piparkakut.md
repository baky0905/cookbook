A Finnish classic from Pargas (Parainen in Finnish) and one of the most popular piparkakku recipes in Finland. The potato starch makes them paler and crisper than ordinary gingerbread, so roll the dough as thin as you can.

These are much closer to Norwegian pepperkaker than American gingerbread: thin and crisp, made with syrup rather than molasses.
