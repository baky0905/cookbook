Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week, or freeze for up to 1 month.

Variations from the book: make the oat mixture with browned butter for a nuttier flavour. Other jam and fruit pairs: raspberry jam with chopped peaches, orange marmalade with diced figs, or guava preserves with chopped strawberries.
