Austro-Hungarian in origin: the story goes that a flustered farmer, cooking for Emperor Franz Joseph, scrambled a pancake she couldn't flip. The book's favourite partner is plum compote, whose tartness offsets the sweet, crunchy shreds. Plum jam or soft prunes also work, and kids love it with melted chocolate.
