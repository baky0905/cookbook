steps:
  - { id: pasta, title: Cook the pasta, duration: about 8 min, text: "Bring water to a boil (a 30 cm sauté pan boils faster), salt it and add the spaghetti. Simmer on high until it still has a little crunch in the middle." }
  - { id: bacon, title: Render the bacon, duration: about 8 min, text: "Heat a large heavy pot over low heat. Add a little olive oil and the bacon and let it render and brown, stirring occasionally." }
  - { id: eggs, title: Mix eggs and cheese, text: "In a separate bowl, mix the cheeses with the whole egg and the 3 yolks." }
  - id: pepper
    title: Bloom the pepper
    text: |-
      When the bacon is golden, lift it out and reduce the heat to medium-low; return any bacon fat to the pot and add the black pepper. Fry the pepper, scraping up the browned bits with a wooden spoon.
      Ladle about 300 ml pasta water into the bacon pot to deglaze. Transfer the spaghetti to the bacon pot, keeping the remaining pasta water for adjusting the sauce.
  - id: finish
    title: Finish the sauce
    text: |-
      Stir 100 ml pasta water into the egg and cheese mixture. Add another 100 ml pasta water to the spaghetti and simmer in the shallow water for about 90 seconds, until al dente.
      Take off the heat and stir in the bacon. Add the egg and cheese mixture and stir very well until the cheese melts. If needed, return to low heat, stirring constantly, until the sauce is velvety; don't overheat or it turns gloppy.
      Top with a pinch of parmesan and black pepper and serve.
