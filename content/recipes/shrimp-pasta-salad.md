From the book's tips: the salad is easy to vary. Try mussels, scampi or scallops instead of shrimp, or pieces of pan-fried fish fillet. Any vegetables you like will work, and so will any pasta shape.

Equipment: a pot over 2 litres, a 1-litre pot and a salad bowl.
