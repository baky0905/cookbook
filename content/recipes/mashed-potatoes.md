steps:
  - { id: boil, title: Boil, duration: 20 min, text: "Put the {{potatoes}} in a large pot with the {{cooking-salt}} and cover with cold water by about 10 cm. Starting cold cooks them evenly, so the outsides don't go mushy before the middles are done. Bring to the boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer: a hard boil breaks floury potatoes up so they soak up water. Cook until a knife slides into the middle of the biggest chunk with no resistance, about 15 to 20 minutes." }
  - { id: milk, title: Warm the milk, text: "While the potatoes cook, warm the {{milk}} in a small pan with the {{garlic}} and {{bay-leaf}} until steaming, without boiling. Take it off the heat and leave it to infuse. Fish out the garlic and bay leaf before using." }

**Keeping warm:** cover tightly and keep near the stove for up to 30 minutes, or set the bowl over a pan of barely simmering water (not touching it) for up to 2 hours.
