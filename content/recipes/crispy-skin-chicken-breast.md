**Serve with** [mashed potatoes](../mashed-potatoes/) and [simple chicken gravy](../simple-chicken-gravy/), made in the same pan while the chicken rests. Spoon the gravy over the potatoes, not the skin.

**What matters most:** dry skin, a medium (not fierce) pan, and the thermometer rather than the clock.
